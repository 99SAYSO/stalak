Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, with no video limit and no account: open stalak.app and edit. Your free videos carry a small moving watermark (the logo and “Clipped with stalak.app”). To never see ads or the watermark again, get Stalak Pro.

How does the ad remove the watermark?

When you export, choose to watch an ad of about 30 seconds: your video comes out with no watermark. It works for that video only, and you can do it again for every video. Depending on your country or the time, there may be no ad available: try again a bit later.

Which plan should I pick?

Stalak Pro if you post often: no more ads or watermark, the 16:9 YouTube video, karaoke, automatic lyrics and every filter. Creator Plus if you want a month of content at once: packs of 5 to 30 different videos from one song, online saving and a real person to help you.

Monthly or yearly, what’s the difference?

None in the app: you get the same features. Yearly costs less: Pro $49.99 a year (47% less than 12 months), Creator Plus $99.99 a year (44% less).

Why are prices in dollars?

Stalak is sold worldwide, so prices are set in US dollars. At checkout, Paddle shows the amount in your currency, with your country’s taxes (VAT in Europe), before you pay.

Do I need an account?

Not for Free. For Pro and Creator Plus, yes: your subscription is tied to your account, which you create with your email or with Google.

Can my price go up?

No: as long as your subscription runs, you keep your price.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes. You cancel from your account and keep your plan until the end of the paid period. If you lose online saving (end of Creator Plus, or a move to Pro), your online projects are no longer accessible, but we keep them for up to 6 months after you last opened them: they come back if you take Creator Plus again.

How do I talk to the Stalak team?

With Creator Plus, tap the help ice cube, then “Talk to a human”: you chat live with the team. If we’re offline, leave your message and your email: we reply within 24 h. On the other plans, the help ice cube answers your questions, and you can always email contact@stalak.app.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. Payments are handled by Paddle.com, our authorized reseller, which also sends the invoices.

What if I don’t like it?

You get a full refund if you ask within 14 days of a payment. See the refund policy.

Are my videos sent to your servers?

No: editing and export happen on your device. Two exceptions, only if you use them: projects you save online (Creator Plus), kept privately in your account, and the audio sent to write your lyrics automatically (Pro and Creator Plus), which is not kept. Details in the privacy policy.
