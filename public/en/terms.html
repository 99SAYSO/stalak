4. Watermark and rewarded ads

On Free, every exported video carries a small, discreet Stalak logo that jumps to a different spot from time to time. It is added on your device at export. It gives Stalak no rights over your video, and doesn’t mean Stalak has checked or endorses its content.

To remove the watermark from a video without subscribing, you can choose to watch a video ad of about 30 seconds (served by Google). An ad watched to the end removes the watermark from that video only. The watermark is only removed by an ad watched to the end or by a subscription: if you close the ad before the end, or no ad is available at that moment, the video keeps its watermark; you can try again later or subscribe. Watching an ad is always your choice: saying no doesn’t stop you exporting your video with the watermark.

The watermark is part of the free plan: don’t remove, hide or crop it out by other means. With Pro or Creator Plus, there’s no watermark and no ads.
