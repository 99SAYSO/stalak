Privacy and cookies

In short: Stalak does not receive your videos or your sound, except the sound of a part when you use “Auto transcription” (it is not kept) and, on Creator Plus, the projects that save to your account. All the editing and the export happen in your browser, on your device. You can use Stalak without an account. There are no ads on Stalak. We don’t sell or share your personal information. US residents: see Your US privacy rights and Your Privacy Choices.

What stays on your device

Your sound, your takes, your lyrics and the exported videos. The site keeps a few settings in your browser (local storage): the lyrics style, the synced lyrics of each sound, the chosen mode, the language, a few technical options. You can erase them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Your account (if you create one)

You only need an account to subscribe. We then keep: your email address, your language, the country you signed up from (worked out by Cloudflare from your connection at that moment; not the city or the IP address, and it isn’t updated afterwards), where your first visit came from (the ?ref= link or campaign tag, or the name of the site or app that sent you, never the full address; kept in your browser until you sign up, then with your account), and if you add them your display name, your profile photo and your password (never in plain text: only a cryptographic hash). To sign you in, we also keep the codes sent by email (as a hash, erased after use or after 10 minutes) and a sign-in session (a “stalak_s” cookie needed for the site to work, valid 60 days, no ads or tracking). If you sign in with Google, Google sends us your email address, your name and your profile photo.

If you subscribe, we receive from Paddle the state of your subscription (plan, active, cancelled, renewal date) and a customer ID, then the amount of each payment and each refund (for our accounts). We never see your card.

Thank-you email (new subscribers). About 3 hours after your first subscription, you get one personal email from 99sayso, the founder of Stalak, sent from 99sayso@stalak.app (through Resend). Only once: never at a renewal, and not again if you subscribe later with the same address. It thanks you and asks 3 optional questions (how you found Stalak, why you chose your plan, what you’d like to see improved); it contains no ad and no offer. Answering is up to you. If you reply, your answer goes to contact@stalak.app and is read by 99sayso in person, only to improve Stalak; we keep replies 12 months at most, then delete them, and never publish or quote them without asking you first. To be sure it’s sent only once, we keep a scrambled form (hash) of your email address, even if you delete your account; it can’t be turned back into your address and is erased after 3 years. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (thanking customers and improving the service). If you don’t want it, or want your replies deleted, write to contact@stalak.app.

Online saving (Stalak Creator Plus). Your projects save to your account automatically: the song, takes, lyrics and edit of each project, with a small picture of the edit used as its cover in the Gallery, are kept privately in your account (up to 100 GB): only you can reach them. You can delete a project at any time in the Gallery (account menu, top right). A project you haven’t opened for 6 months is deleted, after a reminder email sent at least 14 days before.

This data is only used to run your account and subscription (performance of the contract), except the country and where your first visit came from, which only feed our own statistics (where Stalak is used and which posts bring accounts; legitimate interest). It is kept as long as your account exists; when you delete your account from the Account page, it is erased immediately.

Cookies

There are no ads on Stalak and no advertising or tracking cookies. The site only uses the “stalak_s” sign-in cookie (if you have an account) and the local storage described above, which are needed for it to work: that’s why there is no cookie banner. We don’t use them to follow you on other sites.

Your Privacy Choices

We don’t sell or share your personal information, for money or for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, it is respected automatically. If this ever changes, we’ll update this page first and give you a way to say no.

Your US privacy rights

This section is for residents of California and of other US states with consumer privacy laws (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon). It sums up the information above.

  • What we collect (last 12 months): identifiers (email address, account ID, IP address, sign-in cookie); account details (display name, photo, language); commercial information (your plan and payments, from Paddle); internet activity (pages, technical info, how you use the app); approximate location limited to the country (at sign-up and on visits, never the city or precise location); audio you choose to send for “Auto transcription” (not kept). On Creator Plus, the projects saved to your account. We don’t collect sensitive personal information as defined by these laws, and we don’t use any for inferring things about you.
  • Where it comes from: you, your browser, Google (if you sign in with Google) and Paddle.
  • Why: to run Stalak and your subscription, keep it secure, answer you, thank new subscribers and ask for their feedback (see Thank-you email), and understand how Stalak is used.
  • Who gets it: our service providers (Cloudflare, Resend, Paddle, Google for sign-in and email) to run the service. We don’t sell or share personal information in the sense of these laws.
  • How long we keep it: as described in each section of this page (for example account data until you delete your account, help conversations 6 months after the last message).

Your rights: to know what we collect and get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to opt out of sale or sharing (we don’t do either), and not to be treated differently for using these rights (same prices, same service). To use them, email contact@stalak.app from your account’s email address (or tell us how to reach you if you don’t have an account). We may ask a few questions to confirm it’s you, and answer within 45 days. You can use an authorized agent, who must show your written permission. If we refuse your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if we still refuse, you can contact your state’s attorney general.

Children

Stalak is a general-audience service and isn’t directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child under 13 gave us personal information (for example by creating an account), email contact@stalak.app: we’ll delete it.

Our service providers

  • Google: Sign in with Google (if you choose it); receiving emails sent to contact@stalak.app.

Stalak is run from France and some of these providers are in the United States: your data may be processed in both. For people in Europe, transfers are covered by the safeguards of the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework).

What goes through our services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the technical information needed to show the pages (IP address, browser), as for any website.

Automatic lyrics (Stalak Pro and Creator Plus). When you tap “Write my lyrics”, the sound of the chosen part (mono, reduced quality, without the videos) is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the lyrics, which come back to your device. Neither the sound nor the lyrics are kept by Stalak; we only count, per day, the number of transcriptions and seconds of sound (for the usage limits), erased with your account.

Help assistant. When you ask the help ice cube a question, your question, the last messages of the conversation and a little info about the screen (step, messages shown, file names, browser type, end of the technical log) are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, which writes the answer. Stalak keeps neither the questions nor the answers; only counters (per connection, without the IP address in clear, kept briefly, usually one to two days) are used for the usage limits. The answers are written by an AI and can be wrong: never type a password or a code there.

Visit statistics. While the site is open, your page sends a sign of life about every 5 minutes: a random number drawn when the page opens and the site’s language. With it, we record the country the connection comes from, as worked out by Cloudflare at that moment (two letters, like “US”): never the city, the region or the IP address. There is no cookie and no account in it: it is only used to count visits, the visitors on the site right now and the countries they come from. It is kept for 90 days.

Usage counters. A few steps of the app add 1 to a daily counter (for example “video exported”, “plans opened”, or the kind of error when an export fails): only the day and the total. For exported videos, we also count how many each visit makes, linked only to the random number of the visit (see above) and to whether it’s an iPhone or iPad, kept 90 days. These counters hold no cookie, no account, no email and no IP address, and nothing about the video itself.

Anti-robot check. On “Create an account” and “Forgot password”, a Cloudflare Turnstile check may run to block robots that send emails to addresses that aren’t theirs. Cloudflare then processes technical information about your browser and connection (including the IP address) to tell a person from a robot, without advertising cookies.

Limits per connection. To stop abuse, some counts per connection (visits, questions to the help assistant, events) use a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address, never the address in clear, and are kept briefly.

Help

If you write to us for help, you can send us the “technical info” (button in the help bubble): it contains the browser type, the names of your files and technical messages, nothing else. We use it only to fix your problem.

“Talk to a human” (Stalak Creator Plus). In the help bubble, you can write to the team. We keep your messages, our answers, your account’s email address and a little info about the screen at your first message (step, messages shown, file names, browser type), to answer you. If you have left the page, the answer is sent to you by email. Your conversation is erased 6 months after the last message, with your account if you delete it, or sooner on request.

For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection, …): contact@stalak.app. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or your local authority.
