A lip-sync video editor that does the cutting for you

Stalak is a free lip-sync video editor that runs in your browser. Upload your song and a few videos of you performing it, and Stalak cuts between your takes on the beat, keeping your mouth in sync with the track. No download, no account, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Start free, no sign-up

Why it’s different

  • It edits your real takes. No AI face, no fake mouth.
  • Cuts land on the beat: phrase by phrase, on your lines and rhymes, or every bar. You pick.
  • Lyrics on screen, one line at a time for free, word-by-word or karaoke with Pro.
  • Everything runs on your device, so your footage stays yours.

Tips for a clean lip-sync edit

  • Play the song out loud while you film, so every take follows the same audio.
  • Film 3 or 4 takes from different spots: close-up, wide, a different wall or light.
  • Start each take a couple of seconds before the part you want to post.

What’s free, what’s Pro

  • Free: unlimited videos, auto cuts, lyrics one line at a time, 3 filters, 9:16 export. A small Stalak logo, or a ~30-second ad to remove it.
  • Stalak Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr): no ads, no watermark, 16:9 music videos, karaoke and word-by-word lyrics, auto lyrics, all filters and premium effects.

FAQ

What is a lip-sync video editor?

An editor that keeps your performance lined up with the song while it cuts between shots. Stalak does the lining up and the cutting for you: you just bring the takes.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account. Free videos carry a small Stalak logo that moves to a new spot now and then; watch a ~30-second ad to remove it from that video, or go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) for no ads and no watermark.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
