Filming a performance that holds up

Singing shows everything: the breaths, the held notes, the line you push on purpose. A cut in the wrong spot can break that, so Stalak lets you choose how often it cuts.

For ballads and slower songs, pick Every bar or the Chill pace: the shots last longer and the edit breathes with the song. For an upbeat track, Auto with the Normal or Punchy pace keeps it moving. Once your lyrics are synced, On the words switches takes at the start of each line and on the rhyme, which often feels natural with singing.

Every take is lined up with the same audio, so your mouth stays on the words after each cut, held notes included. Sing along at full voice while you film: a quiet lip-sync shows on camera.

Mix your framings: a close-up for the lines that hurt, a wider shot that shows the room or the outfit. Light your face from the front (a window works) so every take stays clear.

With Stalak Pro, the lyrics can follow your voice word by word, or light up like karaoke, in any size. For a cover, check that you’re allowed to post the song on the platform.

Tips for a singing performance video

  • For ballads, pick “Every bar” or the Chill pace instead of quick cuts. To cut on the lines, sync your lyrics first.
  • Mix close-ups and wide shots so the emotion and the setting both show.
  • For covers, only post songs you’re allowed to use on the platform.

FAQ

Does it work for slow songs?

Yes. Pick “Every bar” or the Chill pace for longer shots, and the edit breathes with the song.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account and no ads. Free videos carry a small watermark (our mascot’s face and stalak.app); go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) to export without it.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
