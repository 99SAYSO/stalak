Performance videos for singers, edited for you

Stalak edits a few takes of you singing into one performance video. Upload your song and your takes; Stalak cuts between them on the beat or on the words, keeps every take in sync and adds your lyrics on screen. It works for covers and originals, free in your browser.

What singers get

  • Cuts on every word or every bar, for slower songs that need longer shots.
  • Karaoke and word-by-word lyrics with Pro, so the words follow your voice.
  • Filters like 35mm Film, Frost and Black & White for free.
  • A full 16:9 video of the whole song for YouTube with Pro.

Tips for a singing performance video

  • For ballads, choose cuts every bar instead of every beat.
  • Mix close-ups and wide shots so the emotion and the setting both show.
  • For covers, only post songs you’re allowed to use on the platform.

FAQ

Does it work for slow songs?

Yes. Choose cuts every bar or on the words instead of every beat, and the edit breathes with the song.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account. Free videos carry a small Stalak logo that moves to a new spot now and then; watch a ~30-second ad to remove it from that video, or go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) for no ads and no watermark.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
