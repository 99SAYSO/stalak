How to make a lip-sync video in 3 steps

To make a lip-sync video, film a few takes of yourself performing the song, then line them up with the audio and cut between them on the beat. Stalak does the lining up and the cutting for you: upload the song, add your takes, export. It’s free and runs in your browser.

Start free, no sign-up

The 3 steps

  1. 1. Upload your song, or the part you want to post.
  2. 2. Add your takes: 2 or more videos of you performing it, filmed before or right in Stalak’s camera.
  3. 3. Pick the cut rhythm (beats, words or bars), add lyrics if you want, and export for TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

Filming tips

  • Play the song out loud while you film each take.
  • Film vertically for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
  • Do at least 3 takes from different angles; more takes means more variety in the cuts.

FAQ

How many takes do I need?

At least 2. Three or four takes from different angles give the best-looking cuts.

Can I film right in the browser?

Yes. Stalak has a built-in camera, so you can film your takes right in the browser.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account. Free videos carry a small Stalak logo that moves to a new spot now and then; watch a ~30-second ad to remove it from that video, or go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) for no ads and no watermark.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
