Edit multiple takes to the beat, automatically

Stalak finds the beat of your song and cuts between your takes automatically. Choose cuts on every beat, on every word or every bar, and Stalak keeps each take in sync with the audio. No timeline to drag, no frame-by-frame editing. Free, right in your browser.

Start free, no sign-up

What “automatic” means here

  • Stalak hears the beat and the words of your song and places the cuts for you.
  • Every take stays lined up with the audio, so the lip sync holds after each cut.
  • Don’t like the result? Pick another cut rhythm and get a new edit.
  • Creator Plus makes 5 to 30 different edits of the same song at once.

Tips for beat-synced edits

  • Fast songs: cuts every beat or every bar both work; try both.
  • Use takes with different framing so each cut is easy to see.
  • Keep the same audio playing while filming every take.

FAQ

Do I need editing software?

No. Everything happens in your browser on iPhone, Android or desktop. There’s nothing to install.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account. Free videos carry a small Stalak logo that moves to a new spot now and then; watch a ~30-second ad to remove it from that video, or go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) for no ads and no watermark.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
