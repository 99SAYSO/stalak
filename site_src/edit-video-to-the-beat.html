How Stalak finds the beat

When you import a song, Stalak listens for its pulse: the kicks, the snares, the accents that come back. From them it works out the tempo, in beats per minute, and where each bar starts. Every cut snaps to that grid.

Most songs are read right away. Some throw it off: a long intro with no drums, a beat that switches halfway, a half-time feel. If the cuts don’t land, open “Cuts not on the beat?” under the song, tap TAP on every beat for a few bars, starting on a strong one, and apply. Stalak fine-tunes your taps on the song itself, then plays beeps over the music so you can check.

With the grid right, the cut mode decides where the takes change. Auto follows the music phrase by phrase. On the words cuts at line starts and rhymes, always on a beat, once your lyrics are synced. Every bar switches takes every bar, in turn. The pace stretches or tightens the shots, from Chill to Punchy.

Wherever a cut lands, each take stays lined up with the audio, so the lip sync holds from the first shot to the last.

Tips for beat-synced edits

  • Fast songs: try the Punchy pace (quick cuts, down to every beat) and “Every bar”; keep the one you like.
  • Use takes with different framing so each cut is easy to see.
  • Keep the same audio playing while filming every take.

FAQ

Do I need editing software?

No. Everything happens in your browser on iPhone, Android or desktop. There’s nothing to install.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account and no ads. Free videos carry a small watermark (our mascot’s face and stalak.app); go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) to export without it.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
