How it works

  1. Pick “Music video”, drop in your songStalak takes the whole song. Trim the start and the end if you want.
  2. Add a few takesFilm yourself performing the song with it playing out loud, or film right in Stalak. Every take gets synced to the music.
  3. Pick Subtle or IntenseThe effects land on your drops, choruses and breaks. Tap any effect to change it, remove it or add one, then export.

What you get

  • A Full HD video for YouTube: 16:9, 1920 × 1080, or vertical if you want
  • Cuts on the beat between your takes, with the lip sync locked in
  • 7 effects: white flash, flash + zoom, zoom, shake, black, bursts of shots and slow-mo
  • Lyrics if you want them: paste yours and tap along to sync them, or leave them off
  • The edit happens in your browser: your takes stay on your device (with Creator Plus, your projects are also saved online)

Filming tips

  • Film horizontally to fill the whole frame. Vertical phone takes work too: Stalak shows them whole, in the middle, over a blurred copy of themselves.
  • Film several takes in different places or outfits: the more takes, the more varied your video.
  • Play the song out loud while you film: Stalak uses each take’s sound to sync it.

FAQ

Can I try it for free?

Yes. Pick “Music video” at step 1 and watch your whole music video in the preview, effects and lyrics included, with the small watermark. Exporting the 16:9 video takes Stalak Pro.

What do I need?

Your song (MP3, WAV, M4A, or a video: Stalak uses its sound) and a few takes of you performing it, filmed with the song playing out loud. No download and no sign-up to try it.

Can I use vertical phone takes?

Yes. In a 16:9 music video, a vertical take shows whole, in the middle of the frame, over a blurred copy of itself. Horizontal takes fill the whole frame.

Can I add lyrics?

Yes, if you want them. Paste yours and tap along to sync them, or let Stalak write and sync them from your vocals with auto lyrics (Stalak Pro).

Can I also post it on TikTok or Reels?

Pick “TikTok / Reels” at step 1 for a vertical 9:16 video of the whole song or a few bars. That one is free, with a small watermark.
