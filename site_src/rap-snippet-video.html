A week of snippets from one session

A rollout means posting the same song again and again until it sticks. Filming something new every day burns you out, so film once and edit many times.

Day one is the shoot: three or four takes of the snippet, in different spots, with the song playing out loud. In Stalak’s first step, pick the part to post, down to the bar. Start on the hook, or on the bar people will quote.

Then let Stalak make the first edit. Once your lyrics are synced, On the words cuts at the start of each line and on the rhyme, so the switches land on your punchlines. Keep the lyrics on screen too, so people catch the bars with the sound off.

For the next days, tap Shuffle for a new edit, or change the pace: Punchy for the hype days, Chill when the part is calmer. With Creator Plus, the pack generator does the whole week at once: 5 to 30 videos from the same takes, each cut differently.

Post one a day and watch which version people replay and share. When a kind of cut works, keep it for the release-day video.

Tips for a snippet people replay

  • Start on the hook or the hardest bar: the first 2 seconds decide everything.
  • Keep it short; 10 to 20 seconds is often enough.
  • Post a different cut every day instead of the same video twice.

FAQ

How long should a rap snippet be?

There’s no rule, but short snippets that start on the strongest bar are easiest to replay. Stalak lets you pick any part of the song, down to the bar.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account and no ads. Free videos carry a small watermark (our mascot’s face and stalak.app); go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) to export without it.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
