How to tease a new song before it drops

To tease a song, post short videos of its best part before it comes out: the hook or your strongest bars, 10 to 30 seconds, with you performing it. Start a few weeks before the release, post more often in the last days, and give the date in every post. Stalak turns one filming session into a week of different teasers.

Which part of the song should you tease?

The part people will want to hear again: the hook, a punchline, or the first bars of a verse that hits right away. Pick one part and repeat it: hearing the same lines several times is what makes people remember them.

  • Skip the intro: start on a word, not on the beat.
  • Keep a part that ends on a strong line, so the video loops well.
  • Try two parts in the first week, then keep the one people replay.

How long should a song snippet be?

Long enough for the hook, short enough to watch twice: often 10 to 30 seconds. YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels take up to 3 minutes, but a teaser should leave people wanting the rest of the song.

What should you post before a release?

A simple plan: the first snippet three or four weeks before, then more cuts of the same part, then a video a day in release week, and the song everywhere on release day. Give the date in every caption, and share a pre-save link if your distributor gives you one.

  • 3 to 4 weeks before: the first snippet, with the release date.
  • 2 weeks before: more cuts of the same part, from other spots.
  • Release week: a video a day, and a countdown in your Story.
  • Release day: “out now”, with the link in your bio and your Story.
  • After: keep posting the song; now people can stream all of it.

How do you make a week of teasers from one shoot?

Film the part several times, in two or three spots, then cut it differently for each day: another order of takes, another pace, another framing. Stalak does that for you: Shuffle gives a new edit of the same takes, and a pack (Creator Plus) makes 5, 10 or 30 at once.

FAQ

Should I post a snippet before the song is out?

Yes. A snippet lets people hear the song before the release and tells them when it comes out. Post a part you’re sure about, in its final mix.

How long before a release should I start teasing?

A few weeks gives you time to post the part several times without wearing it out. Pick a pace you can keep until release day.

Can I tease a song that isn’t mixed yet?

You can, but the snippet is the version people remember. Tease the final mix when you can.
