FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No video limit, no account and no ads: just open stalak.app and start editing. Free videos carry a small watermark (our mascot’s face and stalak.app). Want your videos without it? Go Pro.

How do I remove the watermark?

Go Pro or Creator Plus: from then on, every video you export comes out with no watermark. On Free, it’s on every video, and videos you exported before subscribing keep it.

What is Music video mode?

Pick “Music video” at step 1, and Stalak edits your takes over the whole song into a 16:9 video for YouTube. You can trim the start and the end, or export it vertical too. Effects land on the big moments of your song (drops, choruses, breaks): pick Subtle or Intense, then tap any effect to change it or remove it. On-screen lyrics are off by default, and you can add yours. Try it free in the preview; exporting the video takes Stalak Pro.

Which plan should I pick?

Stalak Pro if you want the full music video or post a lot: 16:9 music videos for YouTube, no watermark, karaoke lyrics, auto lyrics and every filter. Creator Plus if you want a month of content in one go: packs of 5 to 30 different videos from one song, automatic cloud saving and “Talk to a human”.

Monthly or yearly, what’s the difference?

Same features either way. Yearly just costs less: Pro is $49.99 a year (47% off 12 monthly payments) and Creator Plus is $99.99 a year (44% off).

Why are prices in dollars?

Prices are set in US dollars. At checkout, Paddle shows the final amount, including any sales tax or VAT where you live, before you pay. Outside the US, it’s shown in your local currency.

Do I need an account?

Not for Free. For Pro and Creator Plus, yes: your subscription lives in your account, which you can create with your email or Google.

Can my price go up?

No. As long as you stay subscribed, your price stays the same.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account and keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. If you lose cloud saving (Creator Plus ends, or you switch to Pro), you can’t open your cloud projects anymore, but we keep them for up to 6 months after you last opened them. They come back if you get Creator Plus again.

How do I talk to the Stalak team?

With Creator Plus, tap the help ice cube, then “Talk to a human” to chat live with the team. If we’re offline, leave a message and your email and we’ll get back to you within 24 hours. On other plans, the help ice cube answers your questions, and you can always email contact@stalak.app.

How do I pay?

Credit or debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. Payments are handled by Paddle.com, our authorized reseller, which also sends your receipts.

What if I don’t like it?

Ask within 14 days of any payment and you get a full refund. See our refund policy.

Are my videos sent to your servers?

No. Editing and exporting happen on your device. Two exceptions, only if you use them: your cloud projects (Creator Plus), kept private in your account, and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro and Creator Plus), which isn’t stored. Details in our privacy policy.
