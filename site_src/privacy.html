Privacy and cookies

In short: Stalak does not receive your videos or your sound, except the sound of a part when you use “Auto transcription” (it is not kept) and, on Creator Plus, the projects that save to your account. All the editing and the export happen in your browser, on your device. You can use Stalak without an account. There are no ads on Stalak. We don’t sell or share your personal information. US residents: see Your US privacy rights and Your Privacy Choices.

What stays on your device

Your sound, your takes, your lyrics and the exported videos. The site keeps a few settings in your browser (local storage): the lyrics style, the synced lyrics of each sound, the chosen mode, the language, a few technical options. You can erase them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Your account (if you create one)

You only need an account to subscribe. We then keep: your email address, your language, the kind of device you signed up on (iPhone/iPad, Android or computer, read from your browser’s name), the country you signed up from (worked out by Cloudflare from your connection at that moment; not the city or the IP address, and it isn’t updated afterwards), where your first visit came from (the ?ref= link or campaign tag, or the name of the site or app that sent you, never the full address; kept in your browser until you sign up, then with your account), and if you add them your display name, your profile photo and your password (never in plain text: only a cryptographic hash). To sign you in, we also keep the codes sent by email (as a hash, erased after use or after 10 minutes) and a sign-in session (a “stalak_s” cookie needed for the site to work, valid 60 days, no ads or tracking). When you type a code on a new device, that browser is remembered for 90 days so you aren’t asked again there: a “stalak_d” cookie holds a random token, and we keep only its scrambled form (hash), erased when it expires, when you change your password (for your other devices) or when you delete your account. If you sign in with Google, a “stalak_o” cookie (10 minutes, deleted as soon as you’re back) links Google’s answer to your browser, to block forged sign-ins. If you sign in with Google, Google sends us your email address, your name and your profile photo.

If you subscribe, we receive from Paddle the state of your subscription (plan, active, cancelled, renewal date) and a customer ID, then the amount of each payment and each refund (for our accounts). We never see your card.

Thank-you email (new subscribers). About 3 hours after your first subscription, you get one personal email from 99sayso, the founder of Stalak, sent from 99sayso@stalak.app (through Resend). Only once: never at a renewal, and not again if you subscribe later with the same address. It thanks you and asks 3 optional questions (how you found Stalak, why you chose your plan, what you’d like to see improved); it contains no ad and no offer. Answering is up to you. If you reply, your answer goes to {{EMAIL}} and is read by 99sayso in person, only to improve Stalak; we keep replies 12 months at most, then delete them, and never publish or quote them without asking you first. To be sure it’s sent only once, we keep a scrambled form (hash) of your email address, even if you delete your account; it can’t be turned back into your address and is erased after 3 years. If you delete your account before the email is sent, it is cancelled. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (thanking customers and improving the service). If you don’t want it, or want your replies deleted, write to {{EMAIL}}.

Online saving (Stalak Creator Plus). Your projects save to your account automatically: the song, takes, lyrics and edit of each project, with a small picture of the edit used as its cover in the Gallery, are kept privately in your account (up to 100 GB): only you can reach them. You can delete a project at any time in the Gallery (account menu, top right). A project you haven’t opened for 6 months is deleted, after a reminder email sent at least 14 days before.

This data is only used to run your account and subscription (performance of the contract), except the country and where your first visit came from, which only feed our own statistics (where Stalak is used and which posts bring accounts; legitimate interest). It is kept as long as your account exists; when you delete your account from the Account page, it is erased immediately.

Tips by email (optional)

At the end of a video, if you don’t have a subscription, Stalak may offer once on this device: “📩 Get tips for your next videos by email”. It’s optional: your video is ready either way, and nothing is sent unless you type your email address and tick the box, which is never ticked in advance:

“I agree to get tips for my videos and Stalak news by e-mail (2 a month at most). I can unsubscribe at any time with the link in each e-mail.”

  • Confirmation. We then send you one email (from login@stalak.app, through Resend) with a “Yes, I confirm” button. Your address only counts once you tap it; if you don’t confirm within 7 days, it is erased. If it wasn’t you, just ignore that email: you’ll get nothing else.
  • What we keep. Your email address, the language of the site, the version of the text you agreed to, and the dates you agreed and confirmed: nothing else (no account, no country, no IP address, no video). Legal basis: your consent.
  • What you’ll get. Tips for your videos and Stalak news, 2 emails a month at most.
  • Unsubscribing. Every email has an “Unsubscribe” link: one tap on its button, without signing in, and your address is erased right away. You can also write to {{EMAIL}}.
  • How long. A confirmed address is erased 3 years after you confirmed, unless you unsubscribe before. To give it again later, you tick the box again.

To avoid asking again, your browser remembers that you already answered (local storage “stalak:optin”, which only holds the date). This address is never sold or given to anyone, and isn’t linked to a Stalak account. If you are under 15, ask a parent before ticking the box.

Cookies

There are no ads on Stalak and no advertising or tracking cookies. The site only uses three cookies, all needed for signing in (see “Your account”): “stalak_s” (your session, 60 days), “stalak_d” (this device is trusted, 90 days) and “stalak_o” (Google sign-in, 10 minutes); plus the local storage described above. They are needed for the site to work: that’s why there is no cookie banner. We don’t use them to follow you on other sites.

What goes through our services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the technical information needed to show the pages (IP address, browser), as for any website.

Automatic lyrics (Stalak Pro and Creator Plus). When you tap “Write my lyrics”, the sound of the chosen part (mono, reduced quality, without the videos) is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the lyrics, which come back to your device. Neither the sound nor the lyrics are kept by Stalak; we only count, per day, the number of transcriptions and seconds of sound (for the usage limits), kept 90 days at most and erased with your account.

Help assistant. When you ask the help ice cube a question, your question, the last messages of the conversation and a little info about the screen (step, messages shown, file names, browser type, end of the technical log) are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, which writes the answer. Stalak keeps neither the questions nor the answers; only counters (per connection, without the IP address in clear, erased within 2 days) are used for the usage limits. The answers are written by an AI and can be wrong: never type a password or a code there.

Visit statistics. While the site is open, your page sends a sign of life about every 5 minutes: a random number drawn when the page opens and the site’s language. With it, we record the country the connection comes from, as worked out by Cloudflare at that moment (two letters, like “US”): never the city, the region or the IP address. We also record the kind of device (iPhone/iPad, Android or computer, read from the browser’s name), where this visit comes from (a ?ref= link or the kind of site that sent you, kept for this tab only, in session storage “stalak:ref” and “stalak:vsrc”) and whether this browser already came on another day (local storage “stalak:lastDay”, which only holds the date of the last visit and is erased after 13 months). There is no cookie and no account in it: it is only used to count visits, the visitors on the site right now, the countries they come from and their devices. It is kept for 90 days. Visits, and the app steps described below (exports, plans opened, payment window opened, closed without buying and at which step, payments), are also added up into daily totals by source, country and kind of device: these totals hold no visit number and nothing about any person.

Usage counters. A few steps of the app add 1 to a daily counter (for example “video exported”, “plans opened”, or the kind of error when an export fails): only the day and the total. For exported videos, we also count how many each visit makes, linked only to the random number of the visit (see above) and to whether it’s an iPhone or iPad, kept 90 days. These counters hold no cookie, no account, no email and no IP address, and nothing about the video itself.

Anti-robot check. On “Create an account” and “Forgot password”, a Cloudflare Turnstile check may run to block robots that send emails to addresses that aren’t theirs. Cloudflare then processes technical information about your browser and connection (including the IP address) to tell a person from a robot, without advertising cookies.

Limits per connection. To stop abuse, some counts per connection (visits, questions to the help assistant, events) use a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address, never the address in clear, and are erased within 2 days.
