Privacy and cookies

In short: Stalak does not receive your videos or your sound, except the sound of a part when you use “Auto transcription” (it is not kept) and, on Creator Plus, the projects that save to your account. If you link TikTok, a clip you choose to post goes to TikTok (see TikTok). All the editing and the export happen in your browser, on your device. You can use Stalak without an account. There are no ads on Stalak. We ask for your permission (cookie banner) before using any measurement or advertising tool, and you can say no. We don’t sell your personal information. US residents: see Your US privacy rights and Your Privacy Choices.

What stays on your device

Your sound, your takes, your lyrics and the exported videos. The site keeps a few settings in your browser (local storage): the lyrics style, the synced lyrics of each sound, the chosen mode, the language, a few technical options. You can erase them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Your account (if you create one)

You only need an account to subscribe or to start the free trial. We then keep: your email address, your language, the kind of device you signed up on (iPhone/iPad, Android or computer, read from your browser’s name), the country you signed up from (worked out by Cloudflare from your connection at that moment; not the city or the IP address, and it isn’t updated afterwards), where your first visit came from (the ?ref= link or campaign tag, or the name of the site or app that sent you, never the full address; kept in your browser until you sign up, then with your account), and if you add them your display name, your profile photo and your password (never in plain text: only a cryptographic hash). To sign you in, we also keep the codes sent by email (as a hash, erased after use or after 10 minutes) and a sign-in session (a “stalak_s” cookie needed for the site to work, valid 60 days, no ads or tracking). When you type a code on a new device, that browser is remembered for 90 days so you aren’t asked again there: a “stalak_d” cookie holds a random token, and we keep only its scrambled form (hash), erased when it expires, when you change your password (for your other devices) or when you delete your account. If you sign in with Google, a “stalak_o” cookie (10 minutes, deleted as soon as you’re back) links Google’s answer to your browser, to block forged sign-ins. If you sign in with Google, Google sends us your email address, your name and your profile photo.

Free trial of Stalak Pro. To start the trial you give your payment method to Paddle, not to us: we never see your card. We keep on your account that the trial has been used (yes/no), and the date it ends. So that the trial is offered once per person, and not again with a new account, we also keep a scrambled form (hash) of your email address, even if you delete your account; it can’t be turned back into your address, is used for nothing else, and is erased 12 months after you delete your account. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (preventing abuse of the trial); and performing the contract. To ask for the hash to be erased, write to {{EMAIL}}.

Watermark-removal offer (1 €). Same as the free trial: you give your payment method to Paddle, never to us. We keep on your account that the offer has been used (yes/no) so that it is taken once per account, and we email you to confirm it and 2 days before the first full-price charge (service emails: no offer, no consent needed). Legal basis: the contract and our legitimate interest (preventing abuse).

If you subscribe, we receive from Paddle the state of your subscription (plan, active, cancelled, renewal date) and a customer ID, then the amount of each payment and each refund (for our accounts). We never see your card.

Thank-you email (new subscribers). About 3 hours after your first subscription, you get one personal email from 99sayso, the founder of Stalak, sent from 99sayso@stalak.app (through Resend). To be sure it’s sent only once, we keep a scrambled form (hash) of your email address, even if you delete your account; it can’t be turned back into your address and is erased after 3 years. If you don’t want it, or want your replies deleted, write to {{EMAIL}}.

Online saving (Stalak Creator Plus). Your projects save to your account automatically: the song, takes, lyrics and edit of each project, with a small picture of the edit used as its cover in the Gallery, are kept privately in your account (up to 100 GB): only you can reach them. You can delete a project at any time in the Gallery (account menu, top right). A project you haven’t opened for 6 months is deleted, after a reminder email sent at least 14 days before.

This data is only used to run your account and subscription (performance of the contract), except the country and where your first visit came from, which only feed our own statistics (where Stalak is used and which posts bring accounts; legitimate interest). It is kept as long as your account exists; when you delete your account from the Account page, it is erased immediately.

Your artist profile and the “Stats” analysis

The first time you open “Stats”, a few questions set up your analysis. You can skip them, but without your TikTok link (and your Spotify link if you are already online), “Stats” can’t analyse your music. If you answer, we keep:

  • your artist name and your genres; if you pick “Other”, the sentence you type in the free field “what you do” (kept as you wrote it, with your profile: please don’t write sensitive information about yourself or anyone else there);
  • whether you are already on streaming platforms, and the links or usernames you paste: TikTok, Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Instagram, YouTube;
  • the monthly listeners you tell us (a range, only if you gave no Spotify link: the platforms don’t give us this number) and the number you may enter each week in “Stats”;
  • where you post, what holds you back, the monthly listeners you aim for and your goals.

What it’s for. To build your plan and the messages you see in the app, to draw your curves and write your analysis in “Stats”, and, once combined and without your name, to decide what to build next. We don’t use it for advertising, we don’t sell it, and it is never sent to Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity or TikTok. We never ask for your passwords and never log in to your accounts. The links are only kept in your profile, with these exceptions:

  • Spotify numbers, read once a week. If you gave your Spotify link, we read the figures shown on your public Spotify artist page (monthly listeners, followers, plays of your tracks) once a week, to draw your curves. Anyone can see these numbers. The reading is done for us by a provider, Apify (Czech Republic), which only receives the public address of your artist page (never your name, email or account) and sends the figures back; we then erase the copy it holds. If a reading fails, Stalak says so (“figures not updated this week”) and never shows an invented or estimated number; you can type your listeners yourself.
  • Your Spotify releases. Through Spotify’s official programming interface (API), we read the public list of your albums and singles (type and date) to count your releases per month and know the date of your latest one. Spotify learns nothing about you: we only ask it for the public catalogue of the artist page you gave. We keep the counts and dates, not the covers or the audio.
  • TikTok. The link alone gives us nothing more than the link. Your TikTok statistics only come from linking your TikTok account (see TikTok).
  • YouTube, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Instagram. We only keep the link; we don’t read these pages.

How long. Before you have an account, your answers stay in your browser only. Once you create an account, they are saved to it and kept, with the weekly figures and release counts, until you change or delete them, remove the link concerned (the readings of that platform then stop and its figures are erased), or delete your account, which erases everything. To do it, use your Account or write to {{EMAIL}}. Legal basis: your request (you give the links to get the analysis, and you can stop at any time). Stalak isn’t affiliated with Spotify, TikTok or YouTube.

Tips by email (optional)

At the end of a video, if you don’t have a subscription, Stalak may offer once on this device: “📩 Get tips for your next videos by email”. It’s optional: your video is ready either way, and nothing is sent unless you type your email address and tick the box, which is never ticked in advance:

“I agree to get tips for my videos and Stalak news by e-mail (2 a month at most). I can unsubscribe at any time with the link in each e-mail.”

  • Confirmation. We then send you one email (from login@stalak.app, through Resend) with a “Yes, I confirm” button. Your address only counts once you tap it; if you don’t confirm within 7 days, it is erased. If it wasn’t you, just ignore that email: you’ll get nothing else.
  • What we keep. Your email address, the language of the site, the version of the text you agreed to, and the dates you agreed and confirmed: nothing else (no account, no country, no IP address, no video). Legal basis: your consent.
  • What you’ll get. Tips for your videos and Stalak news, 2 emails a month at most.
  • Unsubscribing. Every email has an “Unsubscribe” link: one tap on its button, without signing in, and your address is erased right away. You can also write to {{EMAIL}}.
  • How long. A confirmed address is erased 3 years after you confirmed, unless you unsubscribe before. To give it again later, you tick the box again.

To avoid asking again, your browser remembers that you already answered (local storage “stalak:optin”, which only holds the date). This address is never sold or given to anyone, and isn’t linked to a Stalak account. If you are under 15, ask a parent before ticking the box.

TikTok (optional)

With Stalak Pro or Creator Plus, you can link your TikTok account to your Stalak account if you want. It is optional: you can always save your video and post it yourself. Stalak only uses TikTok’s official tools (Login Kit and Content Posting API), and you choose on TikTok’s own page what you allow.

  • What we get from TikTok. Your TikTok profile: display name and profile photo (a link to it). Your account’s statistics: followers, likes and number of videos. The list of your public videos with their views and likes. And the access keys (tokens) TikTok gives us so we can act for you.
  • What it’s used for, and only that. (1) Publishing to your TikTok account the clips you choose to post, with the caption and settings you pick, only when you tap “Post”: never automatically. (2) Showing you your statistics in Stalak (“My stats”). Only you can see them: never shown to other users, never used for ads, never combined with other data about you.
  • How it’s kept. The access keys are stored encrypted (AES-GCM) and never shown, even to us; TikTok makes the short key last 24 hours and the renewal key up to 365 days. Your TikTok name and photo link are kept as long as your account is linked, even if your subscription ends, until you unlink it. Your statistics and video list are fetched from TikTok when you open “My stats” and kept 24 hours at most (so we don’t ask TikTok again on every visit), then erased. A video you post is sent to TikTok and not kept by Stalak; we keep its publishing status (date, TikTok’s publish ID, done or error) 30 days, to show you its progress.
  • How to unlink and delete everything. In Stalak: Account → TikTok → “Unlink”: we ask TikTok to cancel our access and erase right away your tokens, TikTok name and photo, statistics and publishing statuses. Deleting your Stalak account does the same. You can also remove Stalak in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions); then our access stops working and we erase what we kept within 30 days. Or write to {{EMAIL}}.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. We never sell, rent or share your TikTok data with anyone, and don’t use it to target ads. Videos you post are then handled by TikTok under its own privacy policy.

Legal basis: your request (the service you asked for, by linking your account). Stalak isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Cookies

Stalak uses two kinds of cookies. Necessary cookies don’t need your permission: “stalak_s” (your session, 60 days), “stalak_d” (this device is trusted, 90 days), “stalak_o” (Google sign-in, 10 minutes) and “stalak_c” (linking TikTok, 10 minutes, deleted as soon as you’re back), plus the local storage described above and a small entry that remembers your cookie choice (6 months). Optional cookies are only used if you click “Accept” in the banner: nothing is placed or loaded before you choose. “Refuse” is as easy as “Accept”, and Stalak works exactly the same if you refuse.

  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft, United States): records how pages are used (clicks, taps, scrolling, what is on the screen) as replays and heatmaps, so we can see where people get stuck. What you type in fields (email, password, display name) and the text of your lyrics are masked, and your videos, photos and sound are never recorded. Cookies: “_clck” (up to 1 year) and “_clsk” (1 day). Clarity keeps its data 13 months at most.
  • Google Analytics (Google, United States): counts visits and the steps people go through (home page seen, editing started, video exported, plans seen, checkout opened, purchase) to understand what works. Random identifier, no email address or name sent, advertising features off. Cookies: “_ga” and “_ga_…” (13 months). Data kept 14 months at most.
  • TikTok conversion measurement (TikTok): not active yet. When we switch it on, this page and the banner will say so, and it will only apply to people who accepted after that. When a purchase is confirmed, and only if you accepted, our server will tell TikTok that a purchase happened (plan, amount, time) with your email address in scrambled form (SHA-256 hash), so TikTok can tell whether one of our ads led to it. It places no cookie on your device. We use it only to measure and improve our own ads on TikTok.

Your choice. It is kept for 6 months, then we ask again. You can change it anytime with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page: refusing or withdrawing stops the tools from loading from then on. A Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser counts as a refusal. If you are under 16, accept only if a parent agrees. Legal basis: your consent. To have data already collected by Google or Microsoft erased, write to {{EMAIL}}. The counters described under “Visit statistics” and “Usage counters” below are separate: they work without cookies and without personal data, whatever you choose.

What goes through our services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the technical information needed to show the pages (IP address, browser), as for any website.

Automatic lyrics (Stalak Pro and Creator Plus). When you tap “Write my lyrics”, the sound of the chosen part (mono, reduced quality, without the videos) is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the lyrics, which come back to your device. Neither the sound nor the lyrics are kept by Stalak; we only count, per day, the number of transcriptions and seconds of sound (for the usage limits), kept 90 days at most and erased with your account.

Help assistant. When you ask the help ice cube a question, your question, the last messages of the conversation and a little info about the screen (step, messages shown, file names, browser type, end of the technical log) are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, which writes the answer. Stalak keeps neither the questions nor the answers; only counters (per connection, without the IP address in clear, erased within 2 days) are used for the usage limits. The answers are written by an AI and can be wrong: never type a password or a code there.

Visit statistics. While the site is open, your page sends a sign of life about every 5 minutes: a random number drawn when the page opens and the site’s language. With it, we record the country the connection comes from, as worked out by Cloudflare at that moment (two letters, like “US”): never the city, the region or the IP address. We also record the kind of device (iPhone/iPad, Android or computer, read from the browser’s name), where this visit comes from (a ?ref= link or the kind of site that sent you, kept for this tab only, in session storage “stalak:ref” and “stalak:vsrc”) and whether this browser already came on another day (local storage “stalak:lastDay”, which only holds the date of the last visit and is erased after 13 months). There is no cookie and no account in it: it is only used to count visits, the visitors on the site right now, the countries they come from and their devices. It is kept for 90 days. Visits, and the app steps described below (exports, plans opened, payment window opened, closed without buying and at which step, payments), are also added up into daily totals by source, country and kind of device: these totals hold no visit number and nothing about any person.

Usage counters. A few steps of the app add 1 to a daily counter (for example “video exported”, “plans opened”, or the kind of error when an export fails): only the day and the total. For exported videos, we also count how many each visit makes, linked only to the random number of the visit (see above) and to whether it’s an iPhone or iPad, kept 90 days. These counters hold no cookie, no account, no email and no IP address, and nothing about the video itself.

Anti-robot check. On “Create an account” and “Forgot password”, a Cloudflare Turnstile check may run to block robots that send emails to addresses that aren’t theirs. Cloudflare then processes technical information about your browser and connection (including the IP address) to tell a person from a robot, without advertising cookies.

Limits per connection. To stop abuse, some counts per connection (visits, questions to the help assistant, events) use a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address, never the address in clear, and are erased within 2 days.
