How to make a lip-sync video in 4 steps

To make a lip-sync video, film a few takes of yourself performing the song, then line them up with the audio and cut between them on the beat. Stalak does the lining up and the cutting for you: upload the song, add your takes, export. It’s free and runs in your browser.

Start free, no sign-up

The 4 steps

  1. 1. Upload your song, or the part you want to post.
  2. 2. Add your takes: 2 or more videos of you performing it, filmed before or right in Stalak’s camera.
  3. 3. Add your lyrics if you want: paste them and sync them by tapping, or let Stalak write them (Pro).
  4. 4. Pick how it cuts (Auto, On the words or Every bar) and export for TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

Each step in detail

Your song. Stalak takes an MP3, WAV or M4A file, or a video, and uses its sound. Keep the whole song or just a few bars: drag the handles to set the start and the end.

Your takes. Film each take with the song playing out loud, from the start of the part to the end. Two takes work; three or four look better, from different spots. If you film right in Stalak, the camera plays the song for you while you record.

Your lyrics (optional). Paste one line per row, then sync them by tapping along with the song. Free shows them one line at a time; Stalak Pro adds word-by-word and karaoke styles, and can write and sync them for you.

The edit. Stalak makes a first edit right away. Try Shuffle, change the pace, or tap Keep this shot on the shots you love. If the cuts feel off, open “Cuts not on the beat?” and tap the tempo.

The export. Hit Export: the video is rendered on your device, in full quality from your original files. A longer video takes longer: a whole music video takes a few minutes.

Filming tips

  • Play the song out loud while you film each take.
  • Film vertically for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
  • Do at least 3 takes from different angles; more takes means more variety in the cuts.

FAQ

How many takes do I need?

At least 2. Three or four takes from different angles give the best-looking cuts.

Can I film right in the browser?

Yes. Stalak has a built-in camera, so you can film your takes right in the browser.

Is Stalak free?

Yes. Unlimited videos, no account and no ads. Free videos carry a small watermark (our mascot’s face and stalak.app); go Pro ($7.99/mo or $49.99/yr) to export without it.

Is this an AI lip-sync tool?

No. Stalak never fakes your mouth. It edits your real takes: you perform the song, Stalak picks the cuts and keeps every take lined up with the audio.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, on your device. The only exceptions, if you use them: cloud projects (Creator Plus, saved automatically) and the audio sent for auto lyrics (Pro).
