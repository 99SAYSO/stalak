Questions

One free video a day: how does it work?

With a free account, you export 1 video a day, with a 3-second Stalak logo at the end. The next day, you get a new one. If you want, we email you when your video of the day is ready: one a day at most, and we stop after 7 days without a visit.

Which plan should I pick?

Basic if you want to post as much as you like, with no logo. Pro if you also want the 16:9 YouTube video, packs of 5 or 10 videos, every filter, automatic lyrics and online saving. Max if you release a lot: packs of 30 videos, 60 automatic lyrics a day, 100 GB and a real person to help you.

Monthly or yearly, what’s the difference?

None in the app: you get the same features. Yearly costs less: Basic €39 a year (35% less than 12 months), Pro €109 and Max €139 a year (17% less).

Can my price go up?

No: as long as your subscription runs, you keep your price.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes. You cancel from your account and keep your plan until the end of the paid period. If you lose online saving (end of subscription, or a move to a plan without it), your online projects are no longer accessible, but we keep them for up to 6 months after you last opened them: they come back if you take Pro or Max again.

How do I talk to the Stalak team?

With Max, tap the help ice cube, then “Talk to a human”: you chat live with the team. If we’re offline, leave your message and your email: we reply within 24 h. On the other plans, the help ice cube answers your questions.

Are there limits?

Automatic lyrics: 20 a day with Pro, 60 with Max, for songs up to 8 minutes. Online saving: 20 GB with Pro, 100 GB with Max; a project you haven’t opened in 6 months is deleted, after a reminder email 14 days before.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes, prices shown include VAT in Europe. Depending on your country, the final amount (taxes, currency) is shown before you pay.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. Payments are handled by Paddle.com, our authorized reseller, which also sends the invoices.

What if I don’t like it?

You get a full refund if you ask within 14 days of a payment. See the refund policy.

Are my videos sent to your servers?

No: editing and export happen on your device. Two exceptions, only if you use them (Pro and Max): projects you save online, kept privately in your account, and the audio sent to write your lyrics automatically, which is not kept. Details in the privacy policy.
