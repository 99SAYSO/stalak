Privacy and cookies

This is a translation of the French politique de confidentialité, which is the reference version.

In short: Stalak does not receive your videos or your sound, except the sound of a part when you use “Auto transcription” (it is not kept) and the projects you choose to save online (see below). All the editing and the export happen in your browser, on your device. You can use Stalak without an account. On Free, if you choose to watch an ad to remove the watermark, Google serves that ad, with or without cookies depending on your choice (see “Ads and cookies”).

What stays on your device

Your sound, your takes, your lyrics and the exported videos. The site keeps a few settings in your browser (local storage): the lyrics style, the synced lyrics of each sound, the chosen mode, the language, your cookie choice, a few technical options. You can erase them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Your account (if you create one)

You only need an account to subscribe. We then keep: your email address, your language, and if you add them your display name, your profile photo and your password (never in plain text: only a cryptographic hash). To sign you in, we also keep the codes sent by email (as a hash, erased after use or after 10 minutes) and a sign-in session (a “stalak_s” cookie needed for the site to work, valid 60 days, no ads or tracking). If you sign in with Google, Google sends us your email address, your name and your profile photo.

If you subscribe, we receive from Paddle the state of your subscription (plan, active, cancelled, renewal date) and a customer ID, then the amount of each payment and each refund (for our accounts). We never see your card.

Online saving (Stalak Creator Plus). When you tap “Save online”, the song, takes, lyrics and edit of that project, with a small picture of the edit used as its cover in the Gallery, are sent and kept privately in your account (up to 100 GB): only you can reach them. You can delete a project at any time in the Gallery (account menu, top right). A project you haven’t opened for 6 months is deleted, after a reminder email sent at least 14 days before.

This data is only used to run your account and subscription (performance of the contract). It is kept as long as your account exists; when you delete your account from the Account page, it is erased immediately.

Ads and cookies

When there is an ad. Only on Free, and only if you choose “Watch an ad to remove the watermark”. Before that choice, no ad is loaded. With Pro or Creator Plus, there are never any ads.

Who serves the ad. Google (Google Ad Manager) picks and shows the ad, with its advertising partners. To do this, your browser sends Google technical information: IP address, browser and device type, the Stalak page, and the fact that you watched the ad to the end. Google may also read or set advertising cookies and identifiers, depending on your choice below. Stalak only gets from Google the “ad finished” signal that removes the watermark; we don’t see your ad profile or your clicks on the ad.

Your choice. In Europe (European Union, European Economic Area), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent window asks you first. It offers “Accept all”, “Reject all” (as easy as accepting) and “Customize”, with the list of Google’s partners.

  • If you accept: Google and its partners may use cookies and identifiers to measure the ad, fight fraud and show you ads based on your interests.
  • If you reject: you can still watch an ad to remove the watermark. Google then serves a non-personalized or “limited” ad, which doesn’t use advertising cookies; fewer ads may be available.

Your choice is kept for 6 months at most, then you’re asked again. You can change it anytime with the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of the pages. Legal basis: your consent (for cookies and personalized ads); for limited ads, our interest in funding the free plan.

To learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can also set your preferences in My Ad Center.

Other cookies. Apart from ads, the site only uses the “stalak_s” sign-in cookie (if you have an account) and the local storage described above, which are needed for it to work: they don’t require your consent. Stalak sells no data.

Our service providers

  • Cloudflare: hosting of the website, of the accounts database and of the projects saved online (R2 storage); lyrics transcription and help assistant (Workers AI).
  • Google: Sign in with Google (if you choose it); rewarded ads (Google Ad Manager); receiving emails sent to {{EMAIL}}.
  • Resend: sending sign-in emails (your email address and the code) and emails about your account.
  • Paddle.com: payment, taxes and invoices, as our authorized reseller. Paddle processes your payment information under its own privacy policy and keeps billing records for as long as the law requires.

Some of these providers are located in the United States; transfers are covered by the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework).

What goes through our services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the technical information needed to show the pages (IP address, browser), as for any website.

Automatic lyrics (Stalak Pro and Creator Plus). When you tap “Write my lyrics”, the sound of the chosen part (mono, reduced quality, without the videos) is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the lyrics, which come back to your device. Neither the sound nor the lyrics are kept by Stalak; we only count, per day, the number of transcriptions and seconds of sound (for the usage limits), erased with your account.

Help assistant. When you ask the help ice cube a question, your question, the last messages of the conversation and a little info about the screen (step, messages shown, file names, browser type, end of the technical log) are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, which writes the answer. Stalak keeps neither the questions nor the answers; only counters (per connection, without the IP address in clear, kept briefly, usually one to two days) are used for the usage limits. The answers are written by an AI and can be wrong: never type a password or a code there.

Visit statistics. While the site is open, your page sends a sign of life about once a minute: a random number drawn when the page opens and the site’s language. There is no cookie, no IP address and no account in it: it is only used to count visits and the visitors on the site right now. It is kept for 90 days.

Help

If you write to us for help, you can send us the “technical info” (button in the help bubble): it contains the browser type, the names of your files and technical messages, nothing else. We use it only to fix your problem.

“Talk to a human” (Stalak Creator Plus). In the help bubble, you can write to the team. We keep your messages, our answers, your account’s email address and a little info about the screen at your first message (step, messages shown, file names, browser type), to answer you. If you have left the page, the answer is sent to you by email. Your conversation is erased 6 months after the last message, with your account if you delete it, or sooner on request.
