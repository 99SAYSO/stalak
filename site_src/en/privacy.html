Privacy

This is a translation of the French politique de confidentialité, which is the reference version.

In short: Stalak does not receive your videos or your sound, except the sound of a part when you use “Auto transcription” (it is not kept) and the projects you choose to save online (see below). All the editing and the export happen in your browser, on your device. If you create an account, we only keep what is needed to run it.

What stays on your device

Your sound, your takes, your lyrics and the exported videos. The site keeps a few settings in your browser (local storage): the lyrics style, the synced lyrics of each sound, the chosen mode, the language, a few technical options. You can erase them by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Your account (if you create one)

For Stalak Pro, you create an account. We then keep: your email address, your language, and if you add them your display name, your profile photo and your password (never in plain text: only a cryptographic hash). To sign you in, we also keep the codes sent by email (as a hash, erased after use or after 10 minutes) and a sign-in session (a “stalak_s” cookie needed for the site to work, valid 60 days, no ads or tracking).

If you subscribe, we receive from Paddle the state of your subscription (active, cancelled, renewal date) and a customer ID. We never see your card.

Online saving (Stalak Pro). When you tap “Save online”, the song, takes, lyrics and edit of that project are sent and kept privately in your account (up to 20 GB): only you can reach them. You can delete a project at any time in Account → My projects. A project you haven’t opened for 6 months is deleted, after a reminder email sent at least 14 days before.

This data is only used to run your account and subscription (performance of the contract). It is kept as long as your account exists; when you delete your account from the Account page, it is erased immediately.

Our service providers

  • Cloudflare: hosting of the website, of the accounts database and of the projects saved online (R2 storage); lyrics transcription (Workers AI).
  • Resend: sending sign-in emails (your email address and the code).
  • Paddle.com: payment, taxes and invoices, as our authorized reseller. Paddle processes your payment information under its own privacy policy and keeps billing records for as long as the law requires.

Some of these providers are located in the United States; transfers are covered by the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework).

What goes through our services

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the technical information needed to show the pages (IP address, browser), as for any website.

Automatic lyrics (Stalak Pro). When you tap “Write my lyrics”, the sound of the chosen part (mono, reduced quality, without the videos) is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the lyrics, which come back to your device. Neither the sound nor the lyrics are kept by Stalak; we only count, per day, the number of transcriptions and seconds of sound (for the usage limits), erased with your account.

The site does not use advertising cookies and does not sell any data.

Help

If you write to us for help, you can send us the “technical info” (button in the help bubble): it contains the browser type, the names of your files and technical messages, nothing else. We use it only to fix your problem.
